# Slapton-Sands-Coastal-Analysis
Geospatial study of longshore drift and coastal management in South Devon. Uses fieldwork, GIS, and stats to assess how natural processes impact human intervention | Methods &amp; Tools | Fieldwork: 10 CSA profiles, Survey123, Strava | Stats: Spearman's Rank (\(R_{s}\)=0.79, p&lt;0.02) &amp; Chi-Squared | GIS: ArcGIS Online mapping &amp; NVI metrics
